Physical AI Agent#

Physical AI：AI 从屏幕走向物理世界。agent 感知环境、理解指令、规划动作、通过物理身体执行。核心挑战：实时安全约束、物理定律遵循、sim-to-real 鸿沟、长程任务。

一、综述与概念#

  • [2025.01] Generative Physical AI in Vision: A Survey 生成式 Physical AI 综述，覆盖图像/视频/3D/4D 生成在物理智能中的应用
  • [2025.08] Towards Embodied Agentic AI: Review and Classification of LLM- and VLM-Driven Robot Autonomy and Interaction LLM/VLM 驱动的机器人自主性和交互分类综述
  • [2026.05] World Action Models: The Next Frontier in Embodied AI World Action Model 作为 Embodied AI 下一前沿。VLA 学语义泛化但不建模物理世界演化，WAM 显式建模干预下的世界动态
  • [2026.07] Physical AI Governance: From Theory to Practice Across Life Cycle Physical AI 全生命周期治理：实时安全约束、动态环境交互
  • [2026.03] Safety in Embodied AI: A Survey of Risks, Attacks, and Defenses Embodied AI 安全：风险、攻击、防御
  • [2026.06] Safe Embodied AI for Long-horizon Tasks: A Cross-layer Analysis of Robotic Manipulation 长程任务安全的跨层分析
  • [2026.07] Security of World-Model-Based Embodied AI: A Lifecycle of Threats, Defenses, and Evaluation 世界模型驱动的 Embodied AI 安全生命周期
  • [2026.08] What Language Does and What the Evidence Supports: A Functional Role Taxonomy of Language Grounding in Embodied Agents 语言在 Embodied Agent 中的功能角色分类

二、世界模型 (World Model)#

Physical AI 的核心基础设施：物理世界模拟器，用于训练和验证 agent。

世界基础模型#

  • [2025.01] Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform for Physical AI NVIDIA。Physical AI 需要"数字优先训练"：policy model（自身的数字孪生）+ world model（世界的数字孪生）。Cosmos 提供可定制的世界基础模型平台
  • [2026.06] Cosmos 3: Omnimodal World Models for Physical AI NVIDIA。全模态世界模型：统一处理语言、图像、视频、音频、动作序列。混合 Transformer 架构，支持灵活输入输出配置
  • [2025.05] DreamGen: Unlocking Generalization in Robot Learning through Video World Models 4 阶段 pipeline：用视频世界模型生成神经轨迹（合成机器人数据），实现跨行为和环境泛化

World Action Model (WAM)#

VLA 的进化方向：不只观察→动作，还建模"干预下世界如何演化"。

  • [2026.07] From World Action Models to Embodied Brains: A Roadmap for Open-World Physical Intelligence WAM 路线图：连接候选干预与预测后果，是通向开放世界 Physical Intelligence 的路径
  • [2026.07] DSWAM: A Dual-System World Action Foundation Model for Fine-Grained Robot Manipulation 双系统 WAM：视频世界建模提供密集监督 + 语言级规划接口
  • [2026.07] DynaWM: A Base-VLA-Guided World Foundation Model for Moving-Object Manipulation 动态移动物体的操作：VLA+世界模型联合
  • [2026.08] Foresight Without Seeing: Latent Futures for World Action Models 隐式未来预测：不需要显式视频 rollout，用潜在表示预测未来指导动作
  • [2026.08] Keep the Future, Drop the Rollout: RIFT for World Action Models 只需未来表示，不需要迭代视频 rollout。4 个 WAM × 40 个 LIBERO 任务验证
  • [2026.08] Flex-π: A Multi-Stream World-Action Model with Compute Flexibility 多流 WAM：同一视频生成骨干同时预测 RGB+3D几何+物体语义
  • [2026.08] JEPA-WAM: Stage-Level Joint-Embedding Prediction for World-Action Models 阶段级联合嵌入预测：不只预测短期局部未来，还预测阶段级全局未来
  • [2026.08] Surgical WAM: A World-Action Model for Data-Efficient Surgical Robot Learning 手术机器人 WAM：解决动作标注数据稀缺问题

Agent 世界模型#

  • [2026.07] DSWorld: A Data Science World Model for Efficient Autonomous Agents 数据科学世界模型：预测数据操作的效果，减少试错计算
  • [2026.08] FedWorld: Scope-Aware Federation of Agent World Models 联邦世界模型：多个 agent 共享世界知识，scope-aware 合并
  • [2026.06] Active Inference as the Test-Time Scaling Law for Physical AI Agents 主动推理作为 Physical AI 的测试时 scaling law：agent 用世界模型推理应对未见场景

  • [2026.04] HY-Embodied-0.5: Embodied Foundation Models for Real-World Agents 面向真实世界 embodied agent 的基础模型
  • [2026.06] ROBOSHACKLES: A Safety Dataset for Human-Injury Prevention in Embodied Foundation Models Embodied 基础模型的人身伤害预防安全数据集
